# My taste

Rewrite this in your own words. Claude reads it every run to score events 0–10,
together with your recent swipes (feedback/swipes.json).

## Yes
- DIY and independent venues, small rooms, things that feel like a scene rather than a product
- Post-punk, noise, darkwave, synth, experimental electronics, drone
- Media art, hacker culture, tactile/physical tech art, cybernetics, critical talks about technology and AI
- Rare international acts that seldom play Austria — flag these early so I can book
- Readings and talks with an edge; film screenings with a discussion

## No
- Tribute and cover bands, schlager, generic party nights
- Tourist fairs, markets, mainstream festivals
- Anything that is mostly a sponsor event

## Context
- I live in Vienna and often spend weekends in Brunnbach (Ennstal).
- From Brunnbach, Steyr, Linz and Wels are realistic; Vienna only for something special.
- I like being outdoors on dry weekends in Brunnbach.
